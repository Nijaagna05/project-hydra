# Phishing Analysis

## Evidence Reviewed
Three simulated phishing messages were reviewed.

## Indicators
1. Urgent language encouraging immediate action.
2. Sender and reply-to identities may not match.
3. Links point to non-official placeholder domains.
4. Attachment names may disguise executable or archive content.

## Conclusion
The messages demonstrate common phishing indicators. They are fictional training artifacts and not evidence of a real campaign.
