# Legal, Ethical & Impact Analysis

## Indian Legal Framework
Relevant provisions may include provisions of the Information Technology Act, 2000, such as:
- Section 43: unauthorized access/damage in applicable circumstances
- Section 66: computer-related offences where statutory conditions are met
- Section 66C: identity theft
- Section 66D: cheating by personation using computer resources
- Section 72: breach of confidentiality/privacy where applicable

Traditional criminal-law provisions may also apply depending on facts. Current statutory mapping should be verified against the applicable law and facts.

## Global References
The assignment asks for comparison with frameworks such as:
- CFAA (United States)
- GDPR (European Union, primarily data protection)
- Budapest Convention on Cybercrime

These frameworks have different jurisdiction and scope.

## Stakeholder Impact
- Financial loss
- Emotional stress
- Reputational damage
- Operational disruption

## Ethical Issues
- Respect privacy and legal authorization during tracing.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.
- Do not use deceptive infrastructure against uninvolved persons.

## Recommendations
- public phishing awareness
- stronger authentication
- rapid incident reporting
- improved transaction anomaly detection
